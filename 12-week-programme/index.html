The Problem

The Burnout Loop

Most of us with ADHD know this loop: We push hard with systems that don't fit our brains; we crash; we feel exhausted even on days we're not busy; we feel guilty. Then we start over.

Sound familiar?

  • "When there's a deadline, I get it all done. But the next day, I'm depleted."
  • "I'm white knuckling it through my days, forcing myself through every task."
  • "The admin keeps piling up, and I keep putting it off."
  • "I've tried medication, apps, planners, timers and time blocking. Some helped for a while. Yet, none of it lasted."
  • "I know I'm smart and capable. But I'm just tired of fighting myself to get things done."

Trying harder isn't the fix.

Most of us try to fix ADHD with apps, planners and timers. But a system fails when the person running it has nothing left. That's why every new planner works for a week, then stops. So we fix energy first.

The Solution

The Fuel, Focus, Finish Method

Over 12 weeks, we work 1:1 to build a system around how your brain works. There are 3 stages, always in this order:

Stage 01

Fuel (Weeks 1 to 4)

Get your energy back first. Sleep, environment and the first hour of your day, with the First Hour Reset.

Stage 02

Focus (Weeks 5 to 8)

Capture everything, choose what matters, protect the time, and start and finish your work. The Spark Check helps you get started on days when motivation is missing.

Stage 03

Finish (Weeks 9 to 12)

Make it last with The Burnout Loop Breaker which spots the crash before it happens, and your Bad Week Plan means one bad week no longer sends you back to zero.

Weekly 1:1 accountability runs through all 12 weeks. We set up your environment so the right choices become automatic, and you cannot ghost on your own goals, because I will not let you.
