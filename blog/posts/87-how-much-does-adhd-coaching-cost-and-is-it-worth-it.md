# How much does ADHD coaching cost, and is it worth it?

> **Quick answer:** Prices range widely, from around 50 to 150 per session at the lower end to several thousand for a structured multi-month programme. The price tells you very little. What determines whether it is worth it is whether your problem is a doing problem, and whether there is accountability between sessions.


This is general information and not financial advice. Prices vary by country and by individual coach.

## What are the actual ranges?

Three broad tiers exist.

**Single sessions, roughly 50 to 150.** Usually a conversation with no structure around it and nothing between sessions. Cheapest, and in our experience the least likely to change anything, because the problem is rarely a shortage of good ideas.

**Group programmes, a few hundred to around a thousand.** More affordable, and the accountability is diluted across a group. Works for some people.

**One to one structured programmes, often one to several thousand for a few months.** Includes contact between sessions. This is where most ADHD-specific coaching sits.

The field is unregulated, so price does not reliably indicate quality in either direction. An expensive coach is not necessarily good and a cheap one is not necessarily bad.

## What actually decides whether it is worth the money?

Two things, and neither is the price.

**Whether your problem is a doing problem.** If you know what to do and cannot make yourself do it, coaching addresses that directly. If your main difficulty is depression, anxiety or something from your past, the money is better spent on therapy and you will get more from it.

**Whether anything happens between sessions.** This is the one most people do not ask about. A fortnightly conversation with nothing in between is a fortnightly conversation. The reason most of our clients were stuck was that nobody was checking on them, and a session every two weeks does not change that on its own.

| | Likely worth it | Likely not |
|---|---|---|
| Your problem | You know what to do and cannot start | Low mood, anxiety, something unresolved |
| Between sessions | Check-ins, something expected of you | Nothing until the next session |
| Structure | A defined programme with an end | Open-ended conversations |
| The coach | Can say who they are not right for | Takes everyone |

## How should I work out whether I can afford it?

Put a number on what the problem currently costs, which most people have never done.

Unsent invoices. Enquiries you did not reply to. Late fees. The price rise you have not implemented. Work you turned down because you were behind. One client had around five years of unsent billing, somewhere between fifty and seventy-five thousand dollars of work already done.

That number is the comparison, not your monthly budget. For a lot of business owners it makes the decision obvious in one direction or the other.

## What if I genuinely cannot afford it?

Then do not buy it, and do not let anyone talk you into it. A coach pressuring someone with no money is a coach to leave.

Several things cost nothing and address the same mechanism. A weekly call with another self-employed person where you each say what you will do. A group where you state three things and report back. Interim dates given to clients rather than one final deadline.

None of those are as effective as structured one to one work. All of them are considerably better than nothing, and all of them supply the missing ingredient, which is a person who notices.

## FAQ

**Is a cheaper coach worse?**
Not reliably. Ask about what happens between sessions and what their background is, rather than judging on price.

**Should I pay monthly or upfront?**
Whichever you can actually afford without strain. Be wary of pressure to decide on the call itself.

**How long do people usually work with a coach?**
Commonly two to four months for a structured programme. Long enough to change something, short enough to see whether it worked.

**What if it does not work?**
Ask before you start what happens in that case. A clear answer is a good sign in itself.
